Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains how ("TopperBoard", "we"), , collects and uses personal data, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

What we collect and why

  • Listing details: name or brand, link, one-line description, category and bid amount. Purpose: to publish your sponsored listing. These are shown publicly.
  • Payment details: handled by Razorpay. Razorpay collects your phone number, email and payment method; we receive a payment reference, amount, status, and the email and phone you gave Razorpay (used only for receipts, support and refunds, never shown publicly). We never see card numbers or UPI PINs.
  • Messages you send us: your email address and message, so we can reply and handle complaints.
  • Technical data: our hosting, database and font providers may log your IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the site. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

Consent

By submitting a listing and paying, you consent to us processing the data above for these purposes. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us; we will then remove your listing (see the Refund Policy for whether a refund applies). Payment records we must keep by law are not deleted.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that run the site: Razorpay (payments), Supabase (database), GitHub Pages (hosting) and Google Fonts (fonts). Some of these may process data outside India. We do not sell personal data. We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Listing details: while the listing is on the board, and up to 90 days after removal. Payment records: as long as tax and accounting law requires (currently up to 8 years).

Your rights

Children

TopperBoard is for people aged 18 and over and for businesses. We do not knowingly accept listings from children.
