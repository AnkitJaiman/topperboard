Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information TopperBoard collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Listing details you submit: name, link, description, category and bid amount. These are shown publicly once paid.
  • Payment details are collected and processed by our payment partner (Razorpay). We receive a payment reference, amount and status; we do not store card or UPI PINs.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the site working and secure.

How we use it

To display your listing, process payments, prevent fraud, respond to you and meet legal and tax obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with service providers needed to run the site (hosting, database, payments) or when required by law.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or remove your listing details by writing to YOUR_EMAIL@example.com. Payment records are retained as required by law.

Grievance Officer

In accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules made under it, complaints may be sent to our Grievance Officer at YOUR_EMAIL@example.com. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days.
