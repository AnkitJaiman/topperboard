Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Bids are final

A bid is a one-time payment for a sponsored listing that goes live immediately. Being outbid, moving down the board, or changing your mind does not qualify for a refund.

When we refund

  • We reject or remove your listing for breaking our content rules before it gets meaningful visibility, or
  • You were charged but your listing never appeared due to a technical error, or
  • You were charged twice for the same listing.

How to request

Email YOUR_EMAIL@example.com within 7 days of payment with your payment reference. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Cancellation

You can ask us to remove your listing at any time. Removal does not make the payment refundable except in the cases above.
