Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Bids are final

A bid is a one-time payment for a sponsored listing. Being outbid, moving down the board, or changing your mind does not qualify for a refund. A new bid is a separate listing and is charged in full.

When we refund in full

  • We remove or reject your listing for any reason, including a breach of our content rules, or
  • You were charged but your listing never appeared due to a technical error, or
  • You were charged twice for the same listing.

How to request

Email within 7 days of payment with your payment reference (starts with TB, or your Razorpay payment ID). Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Typos

Spotted a typo in your name, link or description? Email us within 7 days and we'll fix it for free. The category and bid amount can't be changed.

Cancellation

You can ask us to remove your listing at any time. Removal at your own request is not refundable; removal by us always is.
