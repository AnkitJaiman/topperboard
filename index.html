FAQ

How is the ranking decided?

Purely by bid amount within each category. The highest bid is #1. If two bids are equal, the earlier one ranks higher. There is no voting, review or algorithm involved.

Why is only the top 3 clickable?

Top 3 profiles get a featured card with a direct link to their page. Everyone else is listed by name and bid. Want the link? Climb into the top 3.

What happens if someone outbids me?

Your profile stays on the board and moves down one place. You can place a new, higher bid any time to climb back.

Do I get a refund if I'm outbid?

No. A bid is a one-time payment for a listing on the board. Refunds are issued only if we remove a listing for breaking our content rules, as described in the Refund Policy.

What is not allowed?

Betting or gambling, adult content, dating or escort services, get-rich-quick or investment schemes, impersonation, hate speech, and anything illegal in India. Such listings are removed.

Will I get an invoice?

Yes. Your payment receipt is emailed by our payment partner. For GST invoices, write to us from the Contact page.
