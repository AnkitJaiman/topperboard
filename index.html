FAQ

Is this a ranking of the best?

No. TopperBoard is advertising. Every listing is a paid, sponsored placement ranked only by bid amount. There is no voting, review or quality check, and a rank is not an award or endorsement.

How is the ranking decided?

Purely by bid amount within each category. The highest bid is #1. If two bids are equal, the one paid earlier ranks higher.

Why is only the top 3 clickable?

Top 3 listings get a featured card with a direct link. Everyone else is listed by name, description and bid. Want the link? Climb into the top 3.

What happens if someone outbids me?

Your listing stays on the board and moves down. To climb back, place a new bid. A new bid is a separate listing charged in full; it isn't added to your earlier bid.

How long does my listing stay up?

For as long as TopperBoard runs. If we ever close the service, we'll announce it here at least 30 days in advance.

Do I get a refund if I'm outbid?

No. A bid buys a listing on the board, not a fixed position. We refund in full if we remove your listing for any reason, if you were charged but your listing never appeared, or if you were charged twice. See the Refund Policy.

Made a typo?

Email us with your payment reference within 7 days and we'll fix the name, link or description for free. The category and bid amount can't be changed.

Do I get an SEO backlink?

Top-3 links are marked as sponsored, as search engines require for paid links, so they don't pass SEO ranking value. You get visibility and clicks from visitors.

Can I say "#1 on TopperBoard" in my own ads?

Yes, if you make clear it is paid, for example "Sponsored #1 on TopperBoard". Don't present it as an award, rating or merit rank.

What is not allowed?

Betting, gambling or real-money gaming, adult content, dating or escort services, investment, trading or crypto schemes, doctors and medical claims, legal services, political ads, impersonation, hate speech, guaranteed-result or "selection" claims, and anything illegal in India. Such listings are removed.

Will I get a receipt or invoice?

Razorpay emails you a payment receipt. Need a bill for your records? Email us your payment reference.
